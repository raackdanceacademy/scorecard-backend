Scoring Formula

How every branch's monthly score is calculated — RAACK Scorify

⚠️ This page explains the scoring logic based on the fields collected in the IT Form and the category weightages used across the dashboards. If your backend's exact formulas differ, share scoring.py and this page can be updated to match it precisely.
900
Maximum possible Final Score, made up of 6 weighted categories minus penalties

💰 Financial Health

Max 225

Measures whether the branch is hitting its revenue targets, collecting fees on time, keeping operating margin healthy, and staying current on its dues (uniforms, salaries, hotel, merchandise).

Revenue Achievement % = Actual Revenue / Revenue Target Payment Discipline % = Average of (Paid / Due) across T-shirt, Salary, Hotel, Merchandise Financial Health = weighted blend of: • Revenue Achievement % • Fee Collection Rate % • Operating Margin % • Payment Discipline % → scaled to 225 points

📈 Student Growth & Retention

Max 225

Tracks new admissions against target, dropout control, batch utilization, and how well trial classes convert into paying students.

Admission Achievement % = New Enrollments / New Student Target Retention % = (Closing Students) / (Opening Students + New Enrollments) Trial Conversion % = Trial Conversions / Trials Conducted Student Growth = weighted blend of: • Admission Achievement % • Retention % • Batch Utilization % • Trial Conversion % → scaled to 225 points

📋 Operations Discipline

Max 135

How reliably the branch runs its planned classes and follows day-to-day operating processes.

Class Delivery % = Classes Conducted / Classes Planned Operations Discipline = weighted blend of: • Class Delivery % • Trainer Attendance % • Attendance Recording Compliance % • CRM/App Usage % • Report Submission Compliance % → scaled to 135 points

⭐ Brand & Quality Compliance

Max 70

Studio hygiene, curriculum adherence, trainer certification, branding/signage standards, and mystery-audit results.

Brand & Quality = average of: • Hygiene Score % • Curriculum Compliance % • Trainer Certification % • Branding Compliance % • Mystery Audit Score % → scaled to 70 points

😊 Customer Experience

Max 70

Parent/student satisfaction, Google rating, complaint resolution rate, referrals, and event satisfaction.

Complaint Resolution % = Complaints Resolved / Complaints Received Customer Experience = weighted blend of: • Satisfaction Score % • Google Rating (normalized out of 5) • Complaint Resolution % • Referrals (normalized) • Event Satisfaction % → scaled to 70 points

📢 Local Marketing & Community Growth

Max 175

Volume of local marketing activity — partnerships, approved social posts, and events/workshops/camps run in the month.

Local Marketing = weighted blend of: • Marketing Activities Completed • Partnerships Activated • Approved Social Media Posts • Events/Workshops/Camps Conducted → scaled to 175 points (each capped at its own target/benchmark)

⚠️ Penalty Flags

Deductions

Certain risk flags reduce the Base Score directly, regardless of category performance.

• Unauthorized Pricing Discount — flat penalty deduction
• False Reporting Detected — flat penalty deduction (higher severity)
• Trainer Misconduct Issue — flat penalty deduction

Final Score = Base Score (sum of 6 categories) − Total Penalty, floored at 0.
